Momentum you can read

The effect samples ScrollTrigger.getVelocity() on every frame and maps that raw px/second figure to a skew angle, clamped to roughly twenty degrees so the type never tears itself apart. A matching vertical stretch makes the lean feel physical.

Because the distortion is driven by gsap.quickTo, the per-frame cost stays tiny — it reuses a single tween instead of spawning a new one on each update. When the scroll settles, a debounced snap-back tween eases the skew and stretch back to rest.

Velocity-mapped

Seamless loop
