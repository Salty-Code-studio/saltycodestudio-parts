Layered Zoom Scroll

SALTY SURF

Scroll

Built to resolve into focus.

Six duplicate panels start scaled down and blurred, then settle to full size and razor sharp as you scroll — a trailing zoom that pulls the eye through depth. Meanwhile the headline parts and the central subject scales up, all driven by a single shared progress value.

Technique

Shared --progress

One ScrollTrigger broadcasts a 0–1 var that scales the subject and splits the type in lockstep.

Depth

Descending scales

Each layer seeds a smaller start scale and heavier blur, then eases home with a depth-staggered offset.

Feel

Smoother inertia

ScrollSmoother adds weighty momentum so the zoom glides instead of snapping to the scrollbar.
