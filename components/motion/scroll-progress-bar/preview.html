Scroll Progress Bar

Watch the bar at the very top fill as you read.

This is a slim, fixed indicator pinned to the top edge of the viewport. It maps document scroll progress to a horizontal fill from 0 to 100%, scrubbed so it tracks your scroll position exactly.

Why it works

It uses a single scaleX transform on a GPU-friendly layer, so it stays smooth even on long pages. ScrollTrigger reports a normalized self.progress value, which maps cleanly onto the fill's horizontal scale.

Common uses

Reading progress on articles, course/lesson completion cues, or just a tasteful flourish on a long marketing page. Point it at a specific <article> to make it a true reading indicator instead of whole-page.

Implementation detail

The fill starts at scaleX(0) with a left transform origin. As progress updates, GSAP tweens the scale toward the current progress with a tiny duration for buttery smoothing without lag.
