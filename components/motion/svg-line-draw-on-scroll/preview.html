Step 01

Prime the stroke

Set stroke-dasharray and stroke-dashoffset to the path's total length so nothing shows yet.

Step 02

Attach a ScrollTrigger

Use scrub: true so progress maps continuously to scroll position — no jumps.

Step 03

Tween offset to zero

GSAP eases stroke-dashoffset down to 0, revealing the stroke from start to finish.

Step 04

Reverse on scroll-up

Because it's scrubbed, scrolling back up un-draws the line just as smoothly.
