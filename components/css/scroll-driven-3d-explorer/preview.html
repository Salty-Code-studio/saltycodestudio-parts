Drag, swipe, or shift-scroll inside the panel. Each card snaps and publishes its own view-timeline on the inline axis.

How the trick works

1

A timeline per card

Every card declares view-timeline: --s3d-card inline, so its progress through the horizontal scroller becomes an animation clock.

2

CSS 3D does the modeling

The box, the hologram cross and the hex tin are plain divs and inline SVG under preserve-3d. A single keyframe spins them 450 degrees.

3

Choreographed captions

Titles clip in on entry and out on exit, captions fade up only near the snap point. All ranges, no listeners, no script.
