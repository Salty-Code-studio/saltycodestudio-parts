Scroll a little

The header above compresses and gains a shadow over the first 150 pixels of scroll. Scroll back up and it grows again, because the animation is scrubbed by the scroll position itself.

In browsers without scroll-driven animation support, the header simply stays sticky at full size with a permanent soft shadow. Nothing breaks and nothing jumps.
