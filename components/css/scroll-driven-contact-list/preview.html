Sand & Surf · Scroll-driven CSS

Rows that fly in,
then fly right out

Each list row rides its own view() timeline: it slides and fades in entering the scrollport, then slides and fades away leaving it. Pure CSS, zero JavaScript.

Needs Chrome/Edge 115+ or Safari 26+. Elsewhere the lists simply render static, fully readable.

The classic contact list

The card on the right is its own scroll container. Scroll inside it and watch every row animate against the card, not the page.

One keyframe set carries its own timeline ranges, so a single animation-timeline: view() declaration handles both directions:

  • entry 0% to 100% row slides up and fades in while entering at the bottom
  • exit 0% to 100% row slides up and fades out while leaving at the top
  • @supports unsupported browsers just see a normal list
