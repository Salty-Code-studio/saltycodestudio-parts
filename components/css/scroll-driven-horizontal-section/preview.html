Three layers, one ride

The whole effect is a tall wrapper, a sticky window, and one keyframe bound to a view timeline.

1

.shs-section

Stretched to 500vh to create scroll room, and tracked by a named view timeline as it crosses the scrollport.

2

.shs-sticky

Sticks to the top of the viewport for the whole ride and hides the horizontal overflow.

3

.shs-track

Translates to calc(-100% + 100vw), so the strip ends with its right edge exactly against the viewport.
