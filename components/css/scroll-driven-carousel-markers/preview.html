Three declarations, zero scripts

The browser links each marker to the scroller's live position. No scroll listeners, no IntersectionObserver, no state to keep in sync.

1

Publish the timeline

The scroller declares scroll-timeline: --sdcm x, turning its horizontal scroll position into a named, reusable progress value.

2

Hoist the scope

Timelines only resolve up the ancestor tree, and the markers are siblings of the scroller. timeline-scope on the component root lifts the name so they can reach it.

3

Slice the range

Each dot's animation-range covers just its slice of the scroll, sized as 100% divided by the slide count. While its slide is in view, the dot holds its lit keyframe.

Built to degrade gracefully

All the timeline wiring sits inside @supports (animation-timeline: scroll()). Browsers without it still get a fully working snap carousel with clickable marker links, the dots simply keep their resting color.

Smooth marker-click scrolling is reserved for visitors without a reduced-motion preference, and every marker keeps a screen-reader label.
