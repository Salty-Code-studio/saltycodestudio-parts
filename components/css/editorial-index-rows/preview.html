Under the hood

Three moves, zero scripts

The whole effect is transitions on custom properties. No observers, no listeners, nothing to initialize.

1

Ink sweep

The label is clipped to a 240% wide gradient with background-clip: text. Sliding background-position pulls full ink across the glyphs. Browsers without support get a clean color crossfade instead.

2

Overshoot arrow

The arrow rests at translateX(-0.7em) and springs to zero through a bouncy cubic-bezier, so it lands with a small, satisfying overshoot.

3

Focus mirrors hover

Every state rides :is(:hover, :focus-visible), so keyboard users get the identical show plus a visible ring. Touch screens keep the arrows on screen at all times.
