    How it works

No JavaScript, honest

The stack you just scrolled through is position: sticky plus the native scroll-driven animations API. The wrapper carries view-timeline-name, and each card's content owns one slice of the exit-crossing animation range, calculated from its index.

Everything is wrapped in @supports (animation-timeline: view()) and a reduced-motion query, so older browsers and motion-sensitive visitors get a calm, fully readable sticky stack instead.
