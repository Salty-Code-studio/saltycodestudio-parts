FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need a data team or SQL to use Tidewell?

No. Funnels, cohorts, and breakdowns are point-and-click. Power users can drop into SQL when they want, but it's never a requirement to get an answer.

How long does setup actually take?

Most teams see their first chart in under ten minutes. If you already stream through Segment or RudderStack, you just point it at Tidewell and your historical events backfill automatically.

Is my data secure and compliant?

Yes. Tidewell is SOC 2 Type II certified and GDPR compliant. Paid plans include a signed DPA, configurable PII redaction, and optional EU data residency.

What happens when I outgrow a plan's event limit?

We never stop collecting your data or hit you with surprise overages. You'll get a heads-up well before the threshold and can upgrade in one click — or stay put and we'll sample gracefully.

Can I cancel or export my data anytime?

Always. There's no lock-in. Export raw events to CSV or your warehouse whenever you like, and cancel from settings with no phone call required.
