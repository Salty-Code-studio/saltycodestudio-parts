Why the Litoral

Engineered for the coast, finished like jewellery

Every detail earns its place. We obsess over the things you only notice after a year of daily use — the lid that never leaks, the grip that never sweats, the finish that never chips.

24 hours cold, 12 hot

Triple-vacuum titanium walls hold the line through a full day at the beach or a red-eye flight. Tested at 38°C ambient.

Truly leakproof lid

A magnetic one-hand cap with a silicone gasket. Throw it in your tote upside down — we dare you. No drips, ever.

Sweat-free grip

A soft-touch powder coat that never beads with condensation, so your desk, your bag and your hands stay dry.

Dishwasher-safe

Top rack, every time. The coating and gasket are rated for 2,000+ cycles without dulling or warping.

Made to be repaired

Replaceable gaskets, caps and straws — shipped free for life. One flask, decades of use, zero landfill.

Free engraving

Add a name, a date or a tiny wave at checkout. Laser-etched, never printed, so it lasts as long as the flask.

The thermal core

We cut the flask in half so you don't have to

Three vacuum-sealed chambers, a copper radiation shield and a titanium inner skin. The result: the flat temperature curve on the right, holding within 2°C across a full work day.
